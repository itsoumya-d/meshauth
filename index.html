Phishing-Proof Architecture

Origin Binding

Credentials are mathematically bound to your exact domain. A phishing site on a different origin will fail signature validation instantly.

Zero Shared Secrets

Unlike passwords or OTPs, no secrets are ever sent over the network. Servers only store public keys.

Hardware Backed

Leverages secure enclaves (TPMs, Secure Enclave) on modern devices to protect private keys from exfiltration.
